# Why there is no front end

Purchasing teams need to approve the right orders, find late deliveries and resolve invoice differences. Those jobs are queries and controlled record changes. The free version runs through a coding agent and produces read-only HTML snapshots and draft paperwork.

A mobile goods-receiving app, barcode capture, staff self-service, drag-and-drop approvals and offline sync give people a different way to work. This base does not provide those interactions. It also has no supplier portal, accounting connection, bank feed, payment facility or attachment archive. Enterprise DNA can build the interfaces and connections your version needs through Omni by Enterprise DNA.

Local PGlite is one process at a time. Shared Postgres needs restricted credentials, individual authentication, role controls, backup recovery and an owner for operations. The recorded actor field is not authentication. HTML output is a snapshot that becomes stale; rerun it before making a decision. Protect files containing supplier or employee details.
